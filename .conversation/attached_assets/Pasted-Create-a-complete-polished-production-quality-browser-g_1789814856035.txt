CORE OBJECTIVE

Flash Focus is a single-player, fixed-duration reaction game based on the classic Stroop-effect challenge.

Normally, the player must select the INK COLOR of the displayed word—not the color named by the word.

Example:
- The word BLUE appears in red ink.
- The correct answer is RED.

During a BORDERLESS SHIFT round, the rule reverses for one round:
- The player must select the color named by the WORD.
- The player must ignore the ink color.

The player should understand the standard mechanic within seconds.
